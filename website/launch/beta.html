Your application data

DionLabs (Davide), reachable at support@dionlabs.ai, uses your email, application time, confirmation of the criteria above, consent version, and review status only to organize this test. Applications are readable by DionLabs and stored separately from Boxie’s encrypted mailbox data using Cloudflare in the EU. Applying does not connect your mailbox or create a Boxie account.

We keep applications for 30 days, then remove expired records in a daily cleanup. Withdrawal removes the active record immediately. Cloudflare recovery copies may retain removed records for up to another 30 days. A keyed, temporary network identifier limits repeated submissions; it expires after 24 hours and is removed in the daily cleanup. We don’t store raw IP addresses or browser details in the application database. Cloudflare still processes ordinary request metadata to operate and protect the site.

Save the private withdrawal link shown after applying, or email support from your application address to withdraw, request access, or correct your details. We aim to handle support requests within 7 days. Consent is optional and can be withdrawn without affecting earlier processing. You can raise concerns with your data protection authority.
