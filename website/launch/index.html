Private by design

Your inbox is personal.
Let’s keep it that way.

Mail stays encrypted in your browser by default. Optional cloud storage receives only encrypted mail. Your vault key is shared through device pairing, rather than handed to our server.

Start with Microsoft sign-in and an encrypted inbox in your browser. Google sign-in is used only for the optional cloud vault and native device pairing. Cloud services process account and connection metadata when enabled.

The optional assistant uses the provider you configure. Mail you ask it to work with may go to that provider. AI is not required to read your conversations.
